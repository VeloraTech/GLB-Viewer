03 / THE ESSENTIALS

Everything you need.
Nothing in the way.

A simple viewing experience, designed around the model instead of the software.

Right-click to open

Register GLB Viewer once, then right-click any GLB file and choose Open With > GLB Viewer.

Desktop works offline

The desktop viewer uses local Three.js assets. The browser demo loads its viewer engine and sample model from the web.

Your files never leave your PC

No upload, no tracking of models, no cloud. We track downloads only, never your files.

Lightweight

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. No React, no build step, no framework. Just open viewer.html and go.
